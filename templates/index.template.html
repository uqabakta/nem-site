Approach

No black boxes, no proprietary data

Public data, always

Every chart draws directly from AEMO NEMweb. Nothing here comes from an internal or proprietary source — so nothing here can be un-shared.

Rebuilt around the clock

A scheduled pipeline pulls the latest dispatch data and regenerates each dashboard automatically, several times a day — no manual updates.

Assumptions on the page

Every dashboard states its method, its exclusions, and the cases where a number shouldn’t be read at all — benchmarks and best-case look-backs are labelled as such, not dressed up as forecasts.
