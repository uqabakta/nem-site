5-minute dispatch prices · data to 20 Jul 2026 06:05 · rebuilt 2026-07-19 20:11

How to read this: the cumulative price is the sum of the last 7 days of 5-minute spot prices. When it crosses the Cumulative Price Threshold (CPT, $2,225,900 for FY2026-27), an administered price cap of $600/MWh applies until it falls back — the market's circuit-breaker. The Market Price Cap is $23,200/MWh this financial year. Spike counts are 5-minute intervals at or above each level. Monitoring, not prediction.
