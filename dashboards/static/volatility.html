5-minute dispatch prices · data to 28 Aug 2026 12:05 · rebuilt 2026-08-28 02:10

How to read this: the cumulative price is the sum of the last 7 days of 5-minute spot prices. When it crosses the Cumulative Price Threshold (CPT, $2,225,900 for FY2026-27 — the same in every region), an administered price cap of $600/MWh applies until it falls back — the market's circuit-breaker. The Market Price Cap is $23,200/MWh this financial year. Spike counts are 5-minute intervals at or above each level. Monitoring, not prediction.

Cumulative price vs CPT

Rolling 7-day sum of 5-minute prices. The CPT line is a staircase, not a constant: it is re-indexed to CPI every 1 July, so it steps up each financial year. It is the same value in every region — a NEM-wide reliability setting, not a regional one. Administered pricing ($600/MWh) applies above it.

The CPT sits an order of magnitude above normal trading, so putting both on one axis flattens the actual series into the floor and hides everything worth seeing in it. Fit to data scales to the series and reports the headroom in words; Show CPT puts the threshold on the axis when you want the distance to it. The window high and low are marked.
