Price trend

Monthly average dispatch price (RRP) by region. The structural separation between regions is the interconnector story; the spikes are the trading story.

Negative prices

Share of 5-minute intervals that settled below $0/MWh — the daytime oversupply signal driven by rooftop and utility solar.

FCAS prices

NEM-wide monthly average of the ten frequency-control markets. The 1-second raise/lower pair only begins once the very-fast market opened in late 2023.

Demand

Monthly average and monthly peak operational demand by region.

Peak / off-peak spread

Monthly average peak-window price (Mon–Fri 07:00–22:00; public holidays counted as weekdays) minus off-peak — the raw signal behind the peak-contract premium, and the spread storage and demand-response monetise.

Interconnector balance

Monthly average net interchange per region (AEMO convention: positive = exporting). Watch regions flip from importer to exporter as VRE builds.

Regional price correlation

12-month correlation of 30-minute prices. High cells = coupled regions; a weak cell is where interconnectors bind and spread trades live.
